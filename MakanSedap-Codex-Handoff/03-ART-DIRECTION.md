# Art direction and visual fidelity

The included `assets/concept-board.png` is one original **generated composite**, showing five desktop views and a mobile home concept. It is **not** a pixel-accurate specification, not evidence of working code, and not authoritative for text, dishes, prices, hours or contacts. It serves as a primary *visual* reference.

Primary proposed blend: cinematic charcoal and near-black surfaces, warm copper/terracotta accent, sophisticated ivory serif display, modern legible sans body, amber light, restrained botanical greens and tactile photography. Maintain Malaysian identity through real protected menu, story, material details and imagery—not generic clip art or stereotypes. Ensure mobile hierarchy and readability; avoid embedding text in photographic images.

Before coding, compare the composite against existing repository screenshots and decide what can be preserved. Develop responsive layout rules and visual tokens. Hero should present a clear restaurant headline and menu CTA; five pages should share navigation and footer but have distinctive compositions. Mobile navigation must be keyboard operable; menu cards should be consistent and scan-friendly; gallery modal and reservation demo remain accessible.

Deliver screenshot comparisons at 390px and 1440px for all five routes. Fix clipping, contrast, typographic hierarchy, awkward crop, excess empty space, inconsistent rhythm and unusable controls. Follow reduced-motion preferences and test 200% text resize. Never copy the concept board's fabricated menu prices or booking claims.

Additional distinct creative directions may be developed by Codex from this board and current site; the package contains **one** generated board, not a falsely claimed multi-board set.
