First-round findings repaired: ingredient orbit overflow, leaf contrast 4.49:1, enlarged-text overflow, tiny disclosures, and a caption collision. Browser captures were then repeated. The final raw Home inspector reported two heuristic failures at 1440px, both evaluated below; the reviewed run allows those two rules and has no other failures at 390/768/1440. Raw findings are retained and summarized in taste-review.json.

Contract-scoped exceptions: the cream/chilli palette is explicitly required. Fluid type produces more than five exact computed pixel sizes, while its semantic roles remain consistent. No genuine venue footage exists; generated still art and purposeful user/scroll response carry the motion. The inspector's fast scroll sampler counted three moving elements; independent browser RAF and ScrollTrigger evidence verifies the opening, three-plane bounded scene and three-part finale. The generic count does not justify additional motion. Its covered-text finding is confined to the aria-hidden decorative Studio circle during the required 900ms plate dissolve; real headings and both CTAs remain uncovered. Scene tests record opacity from 1 to 0 at both widths. A neutral photo scrim in Story is the linter's documented gradient exception. Protected long menu headings and real gallery indices are preserved where generic taste rules would prefer shorter labels.

The supplied reference HTML renders its compressed fallback display with overlapping letters on this host. Its art/composition informs V3; those legibility defects and incomplete example records were not copied. Production uses readable real text and the complete authoritative records. On touch widths the LCP plate stays static and two smaller planes carry the same arrival. Final whole-page capture waits for actual finite native animations to finish, so the Studio signature is settled; scene frames retain the real transition.

The visual review is a direct pixel inspection by the primary agent, supported by independent capture/test scripts. It is not a claim of an external jury, physical-device test or accessibility certification. Final release identity and measured performance are recorded in final-report.md.
