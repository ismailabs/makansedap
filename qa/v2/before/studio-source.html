BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few useful answers.

What kinds of websites can you build?

I build business websites, service-led sites and web applications. My work includes automotive, home-service and hospitality concepts, plus Prism, a personal finance product.

Do you work with Malaysian small businesses?

Yes. The portfolio is oriented toward Malaysian SMEs that need clear services, useful mobile experiences and a simple enquiry path.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. I can review your current site, identify where content or interaction gets in the way, and discuss a focused improvement or rebuild.

Will my website work on mobile?

I build responsive layouts and check navigation, content and forms across small, medium and large browser sizes.

What do I need to provide?

Start with your business, audience and the action you want visitors to take. Existing copy, branding, photos and website links help define the scope; any missing content can be discussed first.

How does feedback fit into the process?

I use the scope, content structure and visual direction to guide the build. Feedback and any changes to scope can be discussed at those stages, with the review approach agreed for your project.

Can you include accounts or saved data?

Yes, where the scope calls for them. Prism demonstrates authentication, dashboards and persisted data. Those features need a different scope from a simple service website.

Can you add WhatsApp and help with deployment?

Yes. This portfolio demonstrates a WhatsApp enquiry flow and Vercel deployment. Domain connection and deployment requirements can be included in the agreed scope.

What happens after I contact you?

Send a WhatsApp message about your business, the idea and your budget, even if you’re unsure. I can discuss the requirements with you and clarify the scope before a quote. You can review the form’s message before sending.
