04 GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
worth knowing.

Is MakanSedap a real restaurant?

No. This is a fictional website concept designed by ISMAIL to showcase a premium restaurant web experience.

Can I make a real reservation?

No. The booking interface is a local-only demonstration. Any selected date or time is illustrative and cannot reserve a table.

Is the restaurant halal certified?

There is no operating restaurant or certification. The illustrative menu is designed without pork or alcohol, but it makes no halal certification claim.
