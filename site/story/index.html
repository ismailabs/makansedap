Roots.

Two words. One invitation.

Makan
To eat. An everyday invitation to come together.
Sedap
Delicious. The feeling that keeps you at the table.

MakanSedap brings two ideas to one table: the care of a steakhouse grill and the bold, generous flavours of Malaysian cooking.

Here, a chargrilled ribeye finds its companion in Sarawak black pepper. Percik chicken carries the warmth of coconut and spice. Nasi lemak sits comfortably alongside a beautiful cut of steak.

Table.

Good company is always on the menu.

An ordinary evening can be an occasion in itself. This concept makes room for that: a considered menu, an imagined warm dining room, and enough variety to let everyone find something they love.

This is a fictional culinary story about connection between flavours, traditions and people. There is no operating restaurant. The menu is imagined without pork or alcohol, and makes no halal certification claim.
