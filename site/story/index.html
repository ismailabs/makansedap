PRECISION MEETS MEMORY
01 OUR ORIGIN CONCEPT

The story of
MakanSedap.

MakanSedap was imagined around one simple belief: a remarkable steakhouse should feel as familiar as a meal shared at home.

Inspired by Malaysia’s love of bold spices, slow cooking and gathering around the table, our kitchen brings together the precision of Western grilling with the unmistakable flavours of local cuisine.

Here, a beautifully charred ribeye meets Sarawak black pepper. A flame-grilled chicken carries the warmth of percik spices. Familiar favourites are given thoughtful new expressions without losing what makes them special.

Every dish celebrates the meeting of two ideas: the craft of the grill and the flavours we grew up loving.
